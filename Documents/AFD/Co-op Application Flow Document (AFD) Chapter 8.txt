Chapter 8 — Customer Management Flow
8.1 Purpose

The Customer Management module is the central repository for all customer-related information within Co-op. It enables business owners to maintain detailed customer records, monitor purchasing behavior, build long-term relationships, and gain AI-powered insights into customer trends.

Every customer interaction influences multiple parts of Co-op, including:

Orders
Reports
Dashboard
AI Assistant
Revenue Analytics
Forecasting
Customer Growth Metrics
Cloud Synchronization

The module follows the same offline-first architecture used throughout Co-op, ensuring customer information is always accessible.

8.2 Objectives

The Customer module must:

Store complete customer profiles.
Track purchase history.
Maintain customer notes.
Provide AI-generated customer summaries.
Support fast searching and filtering.
Generate customer analytics.
Synchronize automatically with Supabase.
Operate fully offline.
Prepare for future CRM capabilities.

8.22 Error Handling

Possible customer-related errors include:

Error	Recovery
Duplicate email	Ask the user to choose a different email or merge records
Invalid phone number	Highlight the field and prevent saving
Import validation failure	Show row-level errors and allow correction
Synchronization failure	Save locally and retry automatically
AI service unavailable	Continue without AI summary
Database error	Restore the last valid customer state

The application should never lose unsaved customer data whenever recovery is possible.

8.24 Developer Implementation Notes

Implementation guidelines:

Persist customer changes to SQLite before cloud synchronization.
Link customer records to orders using immutable customer IDs.
Calculate analytics automatically after order updates.
Cache customer summaries for faster profile loading.
Refresh only affected components after edits.
Keep notes separate from customer-facing information.
Design the schema to support future features such as customer groups, loyalty programs, communication history, marketing campaigns, and multiple contact persons.
8.25 Acceptance Criteria

The Customer Management Flow is complete when:

Customers can be created, edited, archived, and viewed.
Purchase history updates automatically after completed orders.
Internal notes can be added and managed.
AI generates contextual customer summaries.
Customer analytics refresh automatically.
Search and filtering operate in real time.
Customer data can be imported and exported successfully.
Offline customer management functions correctly.
Synchronization with Supabase is automatic.
Conflict resolution allows users to choose the preferred version.
Performance targets are achieved.
8.26 Chapter Summary

The Customer Management Flow provides a complete, offline-first customer relationship system for Co-op. It combines structured customer records, purchasing history, AI-generated insights, and real-time analytics into a single workflow that helps business owners understand and serve their customers more effectively. Designed with scalability in mind, the module also establishes the foundation for future CRM features such as loyalty programs, customer segmentation, and marketing automation.
