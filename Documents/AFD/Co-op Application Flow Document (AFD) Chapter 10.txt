Chapter 10 — AI Assistant Flow
10.1 Purpose

The AI Assistant is the intelligence layer of Co-op. Unlike traditional chatbots, the AI Assistant understands the user's current context within the application and assists with business operations rather than simply answering questions.

The assistant is available throughout the application as a floating panel similar to Microsoft Copilot. It can analyze business data, generate reports, explain charts, draft invoices, answer operational questions, and recommend actions—all while ensuring that potentially destructive actions require explicit user confirmation.

The AI Assistant is designed to be a trusted business advisor that enhances productivity without taking control away from the user.

10.2 Objectives

The AI Assistant must:

Understand application context.
Respond naturally to user questions.
Generate business reports.
Summarize business performance.
Explain charts and analytics.
Generate invoices.
Recommend business improvements.
Never perform irreversible actions without permission.
Maintain conversation history.
Integrate with every major Co-op module.

10.16 Error Handling

Possible AI errors:

Error	Recovery
Network unavailable	Inform the user and offer offline history
AI timeout	Retry request
API error	Display friendly message and allow retry
Invalid response	Retry automatically once, then notify user
Context unavailable	Ask the user for additional information

Errors should never cause the application to freeze or crash.

10.17 AI Notifications

The assistant may proactively notify the user when:

Sales decline significantly.
Inventory reaches low-stock thresholds.
Forecast predicts increased demand.
A report is ready.
Synchronization has completed.

Notifications should be informative rather than intrusive.

10.18 Privacy & Security

The AI Assistant follows strict privacy principles.

Only required data is sent to the AI service.
Sensitive business information is minimized where possible.
Authentication tokens are never shared with the AI.
AI responses are not treated as authoritative business records until the user confirms any resulting action.
Conversation history is encrypted when stored locally.

10.20 Developer Implementation Notes

Implementation guidelines:

Use the OpenAI Responses API with GPT-5.5.
Collect contextual information only from the currently relevant modules.
Stream responses where supported to improve perceived performance.
Cache recent AI responses locally for quick access.
Queue failed requests for manual retry rather than automatic replay.
Keep AI interactions separate from business logic so that modules remain functional if AI services are unavailable.
Design the assistant architecture to support future features such as Voice AI, autonomous AI agents, and custom business workflows.

10.22 Acceptance Criteria

The AI Assistant Flow is complete when:

The assistant is available from every major module.
Conversations maintain relevant application context.
Business reports, summaries, invoices, and chart explanations can be generated.
Users are prompted before any save, email, or data-modifying action.
Conversation history is searchable and synchronized.
Offline mode allows viewing previous conversations.
AI failures are handled gracefully without affecting the rest of the application.
Privacy rules are enforced.
Performance targets are met.
10.23 Chapter Summary

The AI Assistant is the intelligent core of Co-op, providing contextual business assistance across every major module. By combining GPT-5.5 with application-aware context, offline-first design, and user-controlled confirmations, the assistant helps business owners make better decisions while preserving trust, privacy, and control. Its modular architecture also positions Co-op for future capabilities such as Voice AI, autonomous agents, and advanced workflow automation.
