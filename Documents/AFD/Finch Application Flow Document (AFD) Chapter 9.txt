Chapter 9 — Reports & Business Intelligence Flow
9.1 Purpose

The Reports module transforms raw business data into actionable insights. It provides business owners with accurate financial reports, inventory analysis, sales performance metrics, forecasts, and AI-generated summaries to support informed decision-making.

Reports are generated from both local and synchronized cloud data, ensuring that users can continue analyzing their business even when offline.

Every report generated in Finch is connected to:

Dashboard KPIs
Orders
Inventory
Customers
AI Assistant
Forecasting
Export & Sharing
Cloud Synchronization
9.2 Objectives

The Reports module must:

Generate reports quickly and accurately.
Provide AI-generated explanations and summaries.
Support interactive charts.
Work offline using local data.
Allow exporting in multiple formats.
Allow users to save reports.
Ask permission before saving or emailing reports.
Automatically refresh when business data changes.
Support future scheduled reporting.

9.19 Error Handling

Possible reporting errors include:

Error	Recovery
No data available	Display an empty-state illustration with suggestions
AI unavailable	Show report without AI summary
Export failed	Retry export
Email failed	Save locally and retry later
Chart generation failed	Reload affected chart
Synchronization failed	Queue report for later synchronization

The report data should remain accessible even if auxiliary features fail.

9.21 Developer Implementation Notes

Implementation guidelines:

Generate reports from SQLite first to support offline use.
Perform expensive calculations asynchronously to keep the UI responsive.
Cache recently generated reports for faster reopening.
Use Recharts for all interactive visualizations.
Integrate AI summaries through the OpenAI Responses API using only the necessary report context.
Generate exports using reusable templates consistent with Finch branding.
Trigger automatic report refreshes after relevant business events.
Design the reporting engine to support future scheduled reports, report templates, and custom dashboards.
9.22 Acceptance Criteria

The Reports & Business Intelligence Flow is complete when:

All four report types generate correctly.
Interactive charts display accurate data.
AI produces meaningful report summaries and chart explanations.
Reports can be saved after user confirmation.
Users are prompted before emailing reports.
PDF, Excel, and CSV exports work correctly.
Reports function offline using local data.
Synchronization with Supabase occurs automatically.
Reports refresh after relevant business changes.
Performance targets are consistently achieved.
9.23 Chapter Summary

The Reports & Business Intelligence Flow provides Finch users with fast, reliable, and insightful reporting capabilities. By combining interactive analytics, AI-powered explanations, hybrid forecasting, and offline-first operation, the module transforms operational data into practical business intelligence. Its scalable architecture also prepares Finch for future enhancements such as scheduled reporting, customizable dashboards, and advanced predictive analytics.
