Chapter 14 — Notifications, Tasks & Productivity Tools
14.1 Purpose

The Notifications, Tasks & Productivity Module helps users stay organized by delivering timely notifications, reminders, task management, and productivity tools. It works closely with the AI Assistant to ensure users never miss important business events while minimizing distractions through intelligent prioritization.

The module integrates with:

Dashboard
Orders
Inventory
Customers
Payments
Reports
AI Assistant
Calendar
Synchronization
14.2 Objectives

The module must:

Deliver real-time notifications.
Support intelligent reminders.
Manage tasks.
Integrate with the dashboard calendar.
Generate AI-powered reminders.
Provide quick actions.
Support recurring tasks.
Allow notification customization.
Work completely offline.
Synchronize across devices.

14.15 AI Productivity Assistant

The AI Assistant helps users stay organized by:

Suggesting daily priorities.
Recommending follow-ups.
Detecting overdue work.
Grouping similar tasks.
Recommending inventory actions.
Suggesting report generation.
Identifying workflow bottlenecks.

Example suggestions:

"Three customer follow-ups are overdue."
"Generate this week's sales report."
"Two suppliers have delayed deliveries."

AI suggestions never create or complete tasks automatically.

14.25 Chapter Summary

This chapter implements Co-op's productivity ecosystem by combining intelligent notifications, comprehensive task management, calendar integration, reminders, and AI-powered workflow assistance. The result is a system that keeps users informed, organized, and productive without becoming intrusive. Through offline support, synchronization, customizable preferences, and background scheduling, the module ensures that important business activities are always visible and actionable.
