Chapter 8 — Customer Management Module
8.1 Purpose

The Customer Management Module enables businesses to build, organize, and maintain long-term relationships with their customers. It centralizes customer information, purchase history, communication records, notes, and AI-driven insights into a single, easy-to-use workspace.

Following the approved UI/UX specification, this module prioritizes speed, clarity, and intelligent customer management while remaining scalable for future CRM functionality.

8.2 Objectives

The Customer Management Module must:

Store complete customer profiles.
Display customer avatars.
Track purchase history.
Support notes and internal comments.
Organize customers into groups.
Provide advanced search.
Support customer imports and exports.
Generate AI recommendations.
Maintain communication history.
Integrate with Orders, Reports, Dashboard, and AI.

8.24 Chapter Summary

This chapter implements Finch's Customer Management Module, providing a centralized workspace for managing customer relationships. Through detailed profiles, purchase history, communication tracking, intelligent organization, and AI-powered insights, businesses gain a complete view of every customer. The module's scalable architecture, fast search capabilities, and seamless integration with the rest of Finch make it a strong foundation for future CRM enhancements.
