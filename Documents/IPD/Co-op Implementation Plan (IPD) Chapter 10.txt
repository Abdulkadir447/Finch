Chapter 10 — Payments & Financial Management
10.1 Purpose

The Payments & Financial Management Module manages all financial transactions within Co-op. It records incoming payments, tracks outstanding balances, maintains an accurate financial ledger, and provides real-time financial insights for business owners.

Following your backend specification, Bank Transfer is the primary payment method for Version 1, while the architecture remains flexible enough to support additional payment methods in future releases.

The module integrates directly with:

Orders
Customers
Reports
Dashboard
AI Assistant
Synchronization
Audit Logs
10.2 Objectives

The Payments & Financial Management Module must:

Record customer payments.
Support partial payments.
Track outstanding balances.
Maintain an immutable financial ledger.
Generate receipts.
Support refunds.
Produce financial summaries.
Synchronize financial records.
Generate AI financial insights.
Prepare for future payment gateways.

10.23 Chapter Summary

This chapter implements Co-op's Payments & Financial Management Module, providing secure and reliable handling of financial transactions from payment recording through refunds and analytics. By combining an immutable financial ledger, real-time balance tracking, receipt generation, AI-powered financial insights, and strong transactional guarantees, the module ensures financial accuracy while remaining scalable for future payment methods and enterprise-level accounting integrations.
