Chapter 7 — Inventory Module Implementation
7.1 Purpose

The Inventory Module is the backbone of Co-op's business operations. It manages products, stock levels, suppliers, categories, variants, serial numbers, stock movements, and inventory analytics.

The module must support businesses ranging from small retail stores to larger organizations while remaining fast, reliable, and easy to use.

7.2 Objectives

The Inventory Module must:

Manage unlimited products.
Organize products into categories.
Support variants.
Track serial numbers.
Track stock movement.
Manage suppliers.
Detect low stock automatically.
Support imports and exports.
Provide powerful search and filtering.
Integrate with AI recommendations.
