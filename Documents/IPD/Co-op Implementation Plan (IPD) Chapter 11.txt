Chapter 11 — Reports & Analytics
11.1 Purpose

The Reports & Analytics Module transforms Co-op's business data into meaningful information that supports better decision-making. It provides interactive dashboards, customizable reports, AI-generated summaries, forecasting, and export capabilities.

Following the application's workflow, users can generate reports at any time. Once a report is generated, Co-op asks the user whether they would like to save it and whether they would like it emailed.

This module integrates with:

Dashboard
Inventory
Orders
Customers
Payments
AI Assistant
Notifications
Synchronization
11.2 Objectives

The Reports & Analytics Module must:

Generate professional reports.
Support interactive charts.
Provide business analytics.
Generate AI summaries.
Export reports.
Support scheduled reports.
Support report templates.
Analyze business trends.
Maintain fast performance.
Scale to very large datasets.

11.23 Chapter Summary

This chapter implements Co-op's Reports & Analytics Module, providing businesses with powerful reporting, interactive visualizations, AI-generated insights, and predictive forecasting. Through customizable reports, scheduled generation, export capabilities, and seamless dashboard integration, users can quickly understand business performance and make informed decisions. The module is optimized for scalability, responsiveness, and future expansion while following the application's user-centric workflow for saving and sharing reports.
