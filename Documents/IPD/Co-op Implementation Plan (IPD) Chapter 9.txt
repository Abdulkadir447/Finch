Chapter 9 — Order & Sales Module Implementation
9.1 Purpose

The Order & Sales Module is the operational center of Co-op, enabling businesses to process quotations, sales orders, invoices, returns, and refunds with speed and accuracy. It integrates directly with Inventory, Customers, Payments, Reports, AI, and Synchronization.

Following the approved UI/UX design, the Orders page uses a split-view interface, with the order list on the left and detailed information on the right, allowing users to manage orders without unnecessary navigation.

9.2 Objectives

The Order & Sales Module must:

Create and manage sales orders.
Support quotations and invoices.
Track the complete order lifecycle.
Handle taxes and discounts.
Process returns and refunds.
Integrate with payments.
Update inventory automatically.
Generate documents.
Support AI recommendations.
Synchronize all order changes.

9.23 Chapter Summary

This chapter implements Co-op's Order & Sales Module, providing a complete workflow from quotation to invoice, payment, and refund. The split-view interface, automated inventory updates, centralized financial calculations, AI-powered recommendations, and comprehensive analytics create a fast and reliable sales management experience. Designed with transactional safety, scalability, and performance in mind, this module forms the operational core of Co-op's day-to-day business activities.
