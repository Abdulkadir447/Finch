Chapter 11 — Payment Management Schema
11.1 Purpose

The Payment Management module is responsible for recording, processing, tracking, and reconciling all financial transactions within Finch. It supports multiple payment methods, partial payments, refunds, payment history, AI-assisted financial insights, and offline payment recording while maintaining complete auditability.

Every payment belongs to exactly one business and is linked to one or more orders.

11.2 Objectives

The Payment Management module must:

Record all payments.
Support multiple payment methods.
Support partial payments.
Support multiple payments for one order.
Process refunds.
Track payment history.
Generate payment receipts.
Support offline payment recording.
Synchronize efficiently.
Integrate with AI analytics and reporting.

11.23 Offline Behavior

The Payment module supports offline operation.

Users can:

Record cash payments.
Record bank transfers.
Generate receipts.
View payment history.
Process eligible refunds (subject to business rules).

Offline payments are stored in SQLite and synchronized automatically when connectivity is restored. Any conflicts are resolved using the application's conflict resolution workflow.

11.24 Developer Implementation Notes

Implementation guidelines:

Use UUIDs for all payment-related tables.
Allow multiple payments per order to support partial payments.
Keep transactions, refunds, receipts, and reconciliation records in separate normalized tables.
Generate payment numbers automatically using the configured numbering settings.
Store receipt PDFs in Supabase Storage and reference them by URL.
Ensure payment status updates automatically based on transaction totals.
Protect all payment data with Row Level Security (RLS).
Record every financial event in the audit log.
11.25 Acceptance Criteria

The Payment Management Schema is complete when:

Payments support cash and bank transfer.
Multiple payments can be applied to a single order.
Partial payment status is calculated correctly.
Refunds update payment and order records automatically.
Receipts are generated and stored successfully.
Users are prompted to save and optionally email receipts.
Payment reconciliation records can be created.
AI generates payment insights.
Offline payment recording synchronizes correctly.
All payment data is secured by RLS and fully audited.
11.26 Chapter Summary

The Payment Management Schema provides the financial backbone of Finch's sales system. It supports secure payment recording, partial payments, bank transfers, refunds, receipt generation, reconciliation, and AI-powered financial insights while maintaining complete auditability and offline-first functionality. Its normalized design, flexible payment model, and integration with Orders, Reports, Customers, and AI ensure that Finch is ready to support both small businesses today and more advanced financial workflows in future enterprise releases.
