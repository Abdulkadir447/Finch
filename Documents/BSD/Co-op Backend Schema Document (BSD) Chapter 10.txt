Chapter 10 — Customer Management Schema
10.1 Purpose

The Customer Management module is responsible for storing and managing all customer-related information within Co-op. It provides a complete view of every customer by combining contact information, purchase history, financial statistics, AI-generated insights, communication history, and future loyalty features into a single unified profile.

Every customer belongs to exactly one business and is protected by business isolation and Row Level Security (RLS).

10.2 Objectives

The Customer Management module must:

Store complete customer profiles.
Maintain customer contact information.
Record purchase history.
Store customer notes.
Support customer groups.
Prepare for future loyalty programs.
Generate customer analytics.
Provide AI-powered customer insights.
Work completely offline.
Synchronize efficiently.

10.23 Offline Behavior

The Customer module fully supports offline operation.

Users can:

Create customers
Edit profiles
Add addresses
Write notes
Assign groups
View purchase history
Search customers

All changes are stored in the local SQLite database and synchronized automatically when connectivity returns.

10.24 Developer Implementation Notes

Implementation guidelines:

Use UUIDs for all customer-related tables.
Keep addresses, notes, groups, analytics, and attachments in separate normalized tables.
Generate customer analytics asynchronously to avoid slowing transactional operations.
Reference orders instead of duplicating purchase data.
Store attachments in Supabase Storage and reference them by URL.
Cache frequently accessed customer summaries locally.
Protect all customer data with Row Level Security (RLS).
Design the schema to support future CRM, loyalty, and marketing automation features.
10.25 Acceptance Criteria

The Customer Management Schema is complete when:

Customer profiles store complete contact information.
Multiple addresses are supported.
Purchase history is derived from orders.
Customer notes and attachments function correctly.
Customer groups support many-to-many relationships.
AI generates customer insights.
Customer analytics are calculated automatically.
Offline functionality works correctly.
Synchronization uploads only modified records.
All customer data is secured by RLS and audited.
10.26 Chapter Summary

The Customer Management Schema provides Co-op with a centralized, scalable customer database that combines profile information, purchase history, analytics, notes, and AI-powered insights into a unified customer view. Its normalized design, offline-first architecture, and deep integration with Orders, Reports, and AI prepare the platform for future CRM capabilities, loyalty programs, targeted marketing, and enterprise customer relationship management while maintaining performance, security, and data isolation.
