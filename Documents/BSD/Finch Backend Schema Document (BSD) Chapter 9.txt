Chapter 9 — Inventory Schema
9.1 Purpose

The Inventory module manages every product, service, stock movement, supplier, and inventory adjustment within Finch. It provides real-time visibility into stock levels while supporting offline operation, AI-powered insights, automatic synchronization, and future multi-location inventory.

The Inventory module integrates with:

Orders
Suppliers
Customers
Reports
AI Assistant
Notifications
Purchasing (Future)
Barcode Scanning (Future)

Every inventory record belongs to a single registered business.

9.2 Objectives

The Inventory module must:

Store products and services.
Support product categories and variants.
Track serial numbers.
Track suppliers.
Record every stock movement.
Support stock adjustments.
Generate low-stock alerts.
Support barcode scanning.
Provide AI inventory insights.
Maintain complete inventory history.
Work offline.
Synchronize efficiently.

9.24 Offline Behavior

The Inventory module fully supports offline operation.

Users can:

Add products
Edit products
Update stock
Create categories
Add suppliers
Assign serial numbers
Record adjustments
View inventory history

All changes are written to the local SQLite database and queued for synchronization when connectivity is restored.

9.25 Developer Implementation Notes

Implementation guidelines:

Use UUIDs for all inventory-related tables.
Keep products, categories, suppliers, variants, and stock movements in separate normalized tables.
Never modify stock directly; all quantity changes must create a corresponding stock_movements record.
Enforce unique constraints on SKU, barcode, and serial numbers within each business.
Generate low-stock alerts automatically when thresholds are reached.
Store product images in Supabase Storage and reference them by URL.
Cache frequently accessed inventory data locally to improve dashboard performance.
Protect all inventory tables with Row Level Security (RLS).
9.26 Acceptance Criteria

The Inventory Schema is complete when:

Products, categories, variants, and suppliers are fully supported.
Serial number tracking functions correctly.
Every stock change creates a movement record.
Low-stock alerts are generated automatically.
Barcode fields support future scanning capabilities.
AI can analyze inventory and generate recommendations.
Inventory operates fully offline and synchronizes correctly.
SKU, barcode, and serial numbers remain unique within each business.
All inventory operations are audited and secured by RLS.
9.27 Chapter Summary

The Inventory Schema forms the operational backbone of Finch's product management system. It provides comprehensive support for products, categories, suppliers, serial numbers, stock movements, and intelligent inventory monitoring while maintaining historical accuracy through immutable stock movement records. Designed around an offline-first architecture and normalized data model, it scales from a single small business to future enterprise deployments with multi-location inventory, advanced purchasing, and AI-driven optimization.
