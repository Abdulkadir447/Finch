Chapter 3 — User Personas & User Journey Maps
3.1 Purpose

This chapter defines who Finch is built for, what they are trying to accomplish, and how the application should support them throughout their daily workflow.

Every feature in Finch must solve a real business problem and reduce the amount of work required from the user.

The primary design philosophy is:

"The software should think before the user has to."

3.2 Primary User
Business Owner

The first version of Finch is designed exclusively for a single business owner.

Future versions will introduce additional roles such as managers and employees, but version 1.0 assumes one authenticated user per business.

Persona Profile
Attribute	Details
Name	Sarah (Example Persona)
Age	30–50
Business Size	Small Business
Employees	1–20
Technical Skill	Beginner to Intermediate
Daily Computer Usage	4–10 hours
Uses Excel	Yes
Current Software	Excel, QuickBooks, Paper Records
Goals

The business owner wants to:

Know how the business is performing.
Keep inventory accurate.
Avoid running out of stock.
Create invoices quickly.
Understand customer behavior.
Save time every day.
Make smarter decisions using AI.
Frustrations

Current pain points include:

Multiple spreadsheets.
Forgotten customer information.
Inventory mistakes.
Manual invoice creation.
Time-consuming report generation.
No clear picture of business health.
Success Definition

The business owner considers Finch successful if:

They understand the business in less than five minutes every morning.
Inventory mistakes decrease.
Reports generate instantly.
AI answers questions accurately.
Administrative work takes significantly less time.

3.7 Navigation Philosophy

Navigation should require minimal thinking.

Every important feature should be accessible within two clicks.

Primary navigation remains visible at all times.

The application should never require deep menu hierarchies.

3.12 UX Principles

Every feature should follow these principles.

Simple

No unnecessary complexity.

Fast

Every interaction should feel immediate.

Predictable

Users should always know what happens next.

Helpful

The AI should proactively provide useful suggestions without becoming intrusive.

Beautiful

The interface should feel premium, trustworthy, and enjoyable to use.

3.13 Empty States

When no data exists, Finch should guide users rather than show empty tables.

Examples:

Orders: "No orders yet. Create your first order to get started."
Inventory: "Add your first product to begin tracking stock."
Customers: "Your customer list is empty. Add a customer or create an order."
Reports: "Generate your first report using AI."
AI Assistant: Suggested prompts such as:
"How can I set up my inventory?"
"What reports can you generate?"
"How do I create an invoice?"
3.14 Error Handling

Errors should be clear, actionable, and non-technical.

Instead of:

"Database Error 500"

Use:

"We couldn't save your order right now. Your data is safe locally and will be synchronized when the connection is restored."

Every error should include:

What happened
Why (if known)
How to fix it
Whether any data was lost (preferably never)
Chapter Summary

This chapter defines who Finch is built for and how users interact with the product. By focusing on a single business owner in v1.0, Finch can deliver a streamlined, intuitive experience centered around quick access to business insights, efficient workflows, and AI-assisted decision-making.
