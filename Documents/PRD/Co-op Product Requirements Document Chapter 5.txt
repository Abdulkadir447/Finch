Chapter 5 — AI Assistant & Intelligent Features
5.1 Overview

The AI Assistant is the defining feature of Co-op. It transforms the application from a traditional ERP into an intelligent business operating system.

Unlike conventional AI chatbots, Co-op AI understands the user's business data, including orders, inventory, customers, sales trends, and reports. Its role is to provide context-aware insights, automate repetitive work, and help users make better business decisions.

Version 1.0 Scope

The AI Assistant will:

Answer business questions
Generate reports
Summarize business performance
Explain charts
Generate invoices
Forecast sales
Recommend inventory actions
5.2 Product Vision

Co-op AI should feel like hiring an experienced business analyst who is available 24/7.

The AI should not simply respond to prompts—it should actively assist users by identifying trends, highlighting risks, and suggesting next steps.

5.3 Design Principles

The AI Assistant must adhere to the following principles:

Context-Aware

Responses should use the user's business data whenever possible.

Action-Oriented

Every answer should conclude with practical recommendations.

Transparent

The AI should clearly indicate when a response is based on actual business data versus general knowledge.

Trustworthy

The AI must never fabricate business metrics or make unsupported claims.

Explainable

Charts, forecasts, and recommendations should include plain-language explanations that users without technical expertise can understand.

5.6 Context Retrieval

Before sending a request to GPT-5.5, Co-op gathers relevant business information such as:

Orders
Customers
Products
Inventory
Reports
Forecasts
Dashboard metrics

Only the minimum required data should be sent to the AI service to protect privacy and reduce costs.

5.7 Prompt Framework

Every AI request follows a structured prompt.

System Prompt

Defines:

Co-op's identity
Response format
Tone
Business rules
Safety constraints
Context Section

Includes only relevant business information.

Example:

Revenue
Inventory levels
Customer metrics
Sales history
User Prompt

The user's question or request.

Example:

"Summarize today's sales."

5.8 Response Format

Every response should follow this structure:

Summary

Direct answer.

Insights

Important observations.

Recommendations

Suggested next actions.

Supporting Data

Relevant metrics.

Optional Visualization

Chart or table when applicable.

5.10 AI Memory

The AI maintains conversation history.

History includes:

User prompts
AI responses
Timestamps

Users can:

Search conversations
Rename conversations
Delete conversations
Export conversations

Business data is not modified based on chat history.

5.11 AI Safety

The AI must never:

Modify business records without explicit confirmation.
Delete data automatically.
Invent financial figures.
Reveal sensitive information outside the authenticated user's scope.
Execute actions without user approval.
5.12 AI Limitations

The AI should gracefully acknowledge when:

Internet connectivity is unavailable.
Insufficient business data exists.
A request cannot be completed.
The question falls outside Co-op's supported capabilities.

Example:

"I don't have enough historical sales data to generate a reliable forecast yet."

5.13 Offline Behavior

When offline:

Chat is disabled.
AI history remains accessible.
Previously generated reports can be viewed.
A clear banner indicates that AI features require an internet connection.
5.14 Performance Requirements
AI response should begin streaming within 2 seconds under normal network conditions.
Complete responses should typically finish within 10 seconds.
Report generation should complete in under 15 seconds.
Context preparation should take less than 500 milliseconds.
5.15 Logging & Analytics

For quality improvement, Co-op records:

Prompt ID
Timestamp
Response duration
Success or failure
Token usage
Model version

Actual business data should not be logged beyond what is necessary for diagnostics.

5.16 Acceptance Criteria

The AI Assistant is considered complete when it can:

Answer natural-language business questions accurately.
Generate all supported reports.
Explain dashboard charts.
Produce business summaries.
Generate professional invoices.
Recommend inventory actions.
Forecast sales using the hybrid forecasting system.
Maintain conversation history.
Export reports and conversations.
Clearly indicate when AI features are unavailable offline.

Chapter Summary

The AI Assistant is the cornerstone of Co-op's value proposition. It combines GPT-5.5 with business context, statistical forecasting, and structured business rules to deliver meaningful, actionable insights rather than generic chatbot responses. Its design emphasizes trust, explainability, and productivity while preserving user control and data privacy.
