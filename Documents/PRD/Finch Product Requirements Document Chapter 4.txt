Chapter 4 — Functional Requirements & Feature Specifications (Core ERP Modules)
4.1 Purpose

This chapter defines the functional requirements for Finch's core ERP modules. It serves as the engineering specification for how each feature should behave, including business rules, acceptance criteria, edge cases, and user interactions.

All requirements in this chapter are considered Must Have for Finch v1.0 unless otherwise noted.

4.10 Business Rules
Orders
Orders cannot contain negative quantities.
Cancelled orders restore inventory.
Completed orders cannot be edited directly; changes require a reversal or refund workflow.
Inventory
Stock cannot become negative unless backorders are enabled.
All stock adjustments require a reason.
Customers
Customers cannot be permanently deleted if they have historical orders.
Historical customer data must remain accessible.
Dashboard
KPIs update automatically after any completed transaction.
Offline data is clearly marked until synchronization completes.
4.11 Error Handling

All modules must:

Display user-friendly messages.
Retry synchronization automatically.
Preserve unsaved work.
Log errors for diagnostics.

No operation should result in silent data loss.

4.12 Non-Functional Requirements for Core Modules
Dashboard loads in under 1 second.
Order creation completes in under 60 seconds.
Search results appear in under 200 milliseconds.
Core modules remain fully functional offline.
Synchronization resumes automatically when connectivity returns.
All user actions are logged where appropriate for auditing.
Chapter Summary

This chapter defines the functional requirements for Finch's core ERP capabilities: Dashboard, Orders, Inventory, and Customers. These modules form the operational backbone of the application, emphasizing speed, reliability, and offline-first behavior.

The specifications in this chapter establish the expected behavior, business rules, and acceptance criteria that developers will use during implementation and testing.
