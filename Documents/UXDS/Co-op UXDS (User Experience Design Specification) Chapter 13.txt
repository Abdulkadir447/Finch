Chapter 13 — Reports & Analytics Screen Specification
13.1 Purpose

The Reports & Analytics module is Co-op's Business Intelligence (BI) center. It transforms raw business data into actionable insights through interactive dashboards, AI-generated summaries, forecasting, and exportable reports.

Rather than simply displaying charts, the module should answer important business questions such as:

How is my business performing?
What products generate the most profit?
Which customers contribute the most revenue?
Where am I losing money?
What should I expect next month?

The Reports module follows four guiding principles:

Clarity — Complex data should be easy to understand.
Insight — AI should explain the numbers, not just display them.
Interactivity — Reports should encourage exploration.
Decision Support — Every report should help users make better business decisions.
13.2 Objectives

The Reports module enables users to:

View business reports
Analyze sales performance
Review Profit & Loss
Monitor inventory performance
Track customer growth
Generate AI summaries
Create forecasts
Export reports
Compare different time periods
Work offline using cached report data

13.30 Acceptance Criteria

The Reports & Analytics module is complete when:

All six report categories are available.
KPI cards, charts, and tables update correctly based on filters and date ranges.
AI generates accurate summaries and contextual insights.
Forecasts display confidence scores and actionable predictions.
Reports can be exported to PDF, Excel, and CSV.
Cached reports remain accessible offline.
Accessibility and performance targets are achieved.
The module integrates seamlessly with Dashboard, Orders, Inventory, Customers, and the AI Assistant.
Chapter Summary

The Reports & Analytics module is Co-op's business intelligence hub, transforming operational data into actionable insights. Through interactive dashboards, AI-generated summaries, forecasting, detailed analytics, and flexible exports, business owners can understand current performance, identify trends, and make informed decisions with confidence. Its responsive design, offline capabilities, and deep integration with the rest of the application make it a central tool for strategic business management.
