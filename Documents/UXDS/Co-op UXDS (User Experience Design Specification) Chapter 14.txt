Chapter 14 — AI Assistant Experience & Conversation Interface
14.1 Purpose

The AI Assistant is the defining feature of Co-op. Unlike traditional business software that requires users to manually search for information, Co-op allows users to interact with their business using natural language.

The AI Assistant serves as a business advisor, analyst, report generator, invoice assistant, forecasting engine, and productivity companion. It understands the user's business context and provides actionable insights rather than generic responses.

Powered by the OpenAI Responses API (GPT-5.5), the assistant combines business data with advanced language understanding to help users make faster and more informed decisions.

14.2 Design Principles

The AI experience follows six core principles:

Context-Aware — Understand the current page and business data.
Action-Oriented — Perform tasks, not just answer questions.
Transparent — Clearly indicate when information is estimated or unavailable.
Fast — Stream responses with minimal waiting.
Non-Intrusive — Available everywhere without disrupting workflows.
Trustworthy — Never fabricate business information.

14.14 Explain Chart

When users request an explanation of a chart, AI should:

Identify trends.
Highlight unusual values.
Explain possible causes.
Suggest business actions.

Example:

Revenue increased steadily throughout the month, with a noticeable spike during the final week. This appears to coincide with higher order volume. Consider increasing inventory for your best-selling products before the next reporting period.

14.22 Error Handling

14.28 Acceptance Criteria

The AI Assistant experience is complete when:

AI is accessible from every major module.
Context-aware conversations work correctly.
Streaming responses are smooth and responsive.
Users can generate reports, explain charts, create invoices, and request forecasts through natural language.
Conversation history synchronizes across devices.
File attachments (PDF, CSV, Excel) are supported.
Offline behavior is graceful and clearly communicated.
Accessibility and performance requirements are met.
All interactions align with Co-op's premium desktop experience.
Chapter Summary

The AI Assistant is the centerpiece of Co-op, transforming the application from a traditional business management tool into an intelligent business partner. Through contextual conversations, streaming responses, actionable recommendations, and deep integration with reports, orders, inventory, and customers, the assistant enables users to interact with their business naturally and efficiently. Its floating interface, dedicated workspace, and robust offline handling ensure a seamless AI experience that enhances productivity without disrupting existing workflows.
