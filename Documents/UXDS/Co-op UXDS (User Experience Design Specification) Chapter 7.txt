Chapter 7 — Dashboard Experience & Workspace
7.1 Purpose

The Dashboard is the central workspace of Co-op.

It is the first screen users see after signing in and serves as the command center for monitoring business performance, reviewing AI insights, managing tasks, and accessing the most frequently used features.

The Dashboard should answer one question immediately:

"How is my business performing right now?"

The user should understand the health of their business within 5–10 seconds of opening Co-op.

7.2 Dashboard Goals

The Dashboard should:

Display the most important business metrics.
Surface AI-generated insights.
Highlight urgent issues.
Provide quick access to common tasks.
Reduce navigation to other modules.
Adapt gracefully to different window sizes.
Refresh automatically when business data changes.
7.3 Dashboard Philosophy

The Dashboard follows three principles:

Inform

Show the current state of the business.

Explain

Help users understand why something happened.

Recommend

Suggest meaningful actions through AI.

Rather than showing only numbers, Co-op explains what those numbers mean and recommends what to do next.

7.27 Dashboard Personalization

Version 1.0 offers limited customization.

Users can:

Collapse widget sections.
Choose default date range.
Select preferred chart type.
Remember widget expansion states.

Advanced drag-and-drop customization is planned for a future release.

7.28 Performance Requirements

Dashboard targets:

Metric	Target
Initial Load	<2 seconds
KPI Refresh	<500 ms
Chart Rendering	<1 second
AI Summary	<2 seconds
Widget Interaction	<100 ms

Animations should never reduce responsiveness.

7.29 Accessibility

Dashboard must support:

Full keyboard navigation.
Screen readers.
High-contrast mode.
Zoom up to 200%.
Visible focus indicators.
Color-independent charts where possible.

Every chart should include accessible labels and summaries.

7.30 Acceptance Criteria

The Dashboard is complete when:

Users understand business performance within 10 seconds.
KPI cards update in real time.
Charts are interactive and responsive.
AI provides useful summaries and recommendations.
Quick actions reduce navigation effort.
Empty, loading, and error states are fully defined.
The layout works across supported window sizes.
Accessibility standards are satisfied.
Chapter Summary

The Dashboard is the operational heart of Co-op, combining business metrics, AI intelligence, charts, recent activity, and quick actions into a single workspace. Its balanced layout, contextual recommendations, and responsive design help business owners monitor their operations and take action with minimal effort. The Dashboard establishes the overall tone of the application by making complex business data understandable, actionable, and visually engaging.
