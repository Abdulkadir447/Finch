Chapter 11 — Inventory Module Screen Specification
11.1 Purpose

The Inventory module is the operational center for managing all products and stock within Finch.

It provides business owners with real-time visibility into inventory levels, product performance, inventory valuation, and stock movement while leveraging AI to proactively identify risks and opportunities.

The module is designed around four principles:

Accuracy — Inventory should always reflect the current business state.
Speed — Stock updates should require minimal effort.
Visibility — Important inventory information should always be easy to find.
Intelligence — AI should help users make better stocking decisions.
11.2 Objectives

The Inventory module enables users to:

Add products
Edit products
Delete products
View inventory levels
Monitor inventory value
Track stock movement
Receive low-stock alerts
Import and export inventory
Search and filter products
View AI inventory insights
Work completely offline
Automatically synchronize with Supabase

11.31 Acceptance Criteria

The Inventory module is complete when:

Users can efficiently manage products and stock.
Expandable rows provide detailed product information without disrupting workflows.
Inventory updates propagate immediately to the Dashboard and Reports.
AI generates actionable inventory recommendations.
Import and export workflows are reliable.
Offline changes synchronize automatically when connectivity returns.
Accessibility and performance requirements are satisfied.
All interactions follow the Finch design system.
Chapter Summary

The Inventory module is the operational hub for managing products and stock within Finch. Its table-first layout, expandable product rows, stock adjustment workflows, AI-driven insights, and offline-first architecture enable business owners to maintain accurate inventory with minimal effort. By combining powerful data management with intelligent recommendations, the module helps users optimize stock levels, reduce shortages, and make informed purchasing decisions.
