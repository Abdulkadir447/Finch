Chapter 12 — Customers Module Screen Specification
12.1 Purpose

The Customers module is the Customer Relationship Management (CRM) center of Co-op. It allows business owners to manage customer information, monitor purchase behavior, maintain communication records, and gain AI-powered insights into customer value and retention.

The module is designed to transform customer data into meaningful business intelligence while keeping daily customer management simple and efficient.

Its guiding principles are:

Organization — Keep all customer information in one place.
Relationships — Understand customer history and engagement.
Insights — Use AI to identify trends and opportunities.
Efficiency — Complete common customer tasks with minimal clicks.
12.2 Objectives

The Customers module enables users to:

Add new customers
Edit customer profiles
Delete customers
Search and filter customer records
View purchase history
Record customer notes
Track spending and activity
Import and export customer data
Receive AI-generated customer insights
Operate offline with automatic cloud synchronization

12.32 Acceptance Criteria

The Customers module is complete when:

Users can create, edit, and manage customer records efficiently.
Customer profiles display complete purchase history and activity.
AI provides meaningful customer insights and recommendations.
Search, filtering, and segmentation perform reliably.
Import and export workflows support CSV, Excel, and PDF.
Offline operation and synchronization function correctly.
Accessibility and performance targets are met.
All components integrate seamlessly with Orders, Reports, AI Assistant, and the Dashboard.
Chapter Summary

The Customers module serves as Co-op's CRM, giving business owners a complete view of every customer relationship. Through a split-view interface, purchase history, AI-powered insights, and offline-first capabilities, users can manage customer information efficiently while making informed decisions to improve retention, increase customer lifetime value, and strengthen business relationships.
