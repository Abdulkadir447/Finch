Chapter 10 — Orders Module Screen Specification
10.1 Purpose

The Orders module is one of the core operational areas of Finch. It is responsible for managing the complete sales lifecycle—from creating an order to generating invoices, updating inventory, notifying customers, and synchronizing business data.

The module is designed around three principles:

Speed — Create and manage orders in under 60 seconds.
Clarity — Present all relevant order information in one place.
Automation — Minimize manual work through AI and system integrations.
10.2 Objectives

The Orders module should allow users to:

Create new orders.
View all existing orders.
Edit and update orders.
Cancel orders.
Generate invoices.
Print invoices.
Export order data.
Search and filter orders.
Perform bulk actions.
Ask AI questions about orders.
Work offline with automatic cloud synchronization.

10.30 Acceptance Criteria

The Orders module is considered complete when:

Users can create, edit, cancel, and view orders efficiently.
Split-view navigation is responsive and intuitive.
Invoice generation is integrated into the workflow.
Search, filtering, and bulk actions are fully functional.
AI provides contextual insights for selected orders.
Offline operation with queued synchronization works reliably.
Accessibility standards are met.
Performance targets are achieved across supported desktop environments.
Chapter Summary

The Orders module is the operational backbone of Finch, enabling business owners to manage customer transactions quickly and accurately. Its split-view layout, integrated invoice generation, AI insights, and offline-first architecture ensure a seamless workflow from order creation to completion. By combining powerful data management with intuitive interactions, the Orders module supports efficient day-to-day business operations while remaining scalable for future enhancements.
