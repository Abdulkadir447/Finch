=== ERP System Database ERD (Entity Relationship Diagram)

Tables and Relationships:

1. USERS
   Primary Key: id
   Attributes: username, email, password_hash, role, created_at
   Relations: 0..* Orders (via customer_id)

2. CATEGORIES
   Primary Key: id
   Attributes: name, description, created_at
   Relations: 0..* Products

3. PRODUCTS
   Primary Key: id
   Attributes: sku, name, description, price, category_id, stock_level, reorder_level, created_at, updated_at
   Relations:
   - Belongs to: 1 Categories
   - 0..* Order_Items
   - 0..* Inventory_Movements

4. CUSTOMERS
   Primary Key: id
   Attributes: name, email, phone, address, company, created_at
   Relations: 0..* Orders (via customer_id) (Note: This is a separate Customer table from Users)

5. ORDERS
   Primary Key: id
   Attributes: customer_id, status, total_amount, created_at, updated_at
   Relations:
   - Belongs to: 1 Customers
   - 0..* Order_Items
   - 1 Status (enumerated: pending, confirmed, shipped, delivered, cancelled)

6. ORDER_ITEMS
   Primary Key: id
   Attributes: order_id, product_id, quantity, unit_price, total_price
   Relations:
   - Belongs to: 1 Orders
   - Belongs to: 1 Products

7. INVENTORY_MOVEMENTS
   Primary Key: id
   Attributes: product_id, quantity_change, reason, reference_type, reference_id, created_at
   Relations:
   - Belongs to: 1 Products
   - Self-referential via reason, reference_type

Key Business Rules:
- 1 Customer can have 0..* Orders
- 1 Category can have 0..* Products
- 1 Product belongs to 1 Category
- 1 Product can have multiple Inventory_Movements
- Each Order has exactly 1 Customer
- Each Order can have 0..* Order_Items
- Each Order_Item belongs to 1 Order and 1 Product
- Foreign keys enforce referential integrity
- Soft deletions NOT implemented (using status flags for Orders)
- Audit trails via created_at timestamps
